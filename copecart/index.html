How is this calculated?

Short version: we estimate two real costs that most digital businesses carry without seeing them on a P&L — checkout friction (cart abandonment from missing payment methods) and compliance overhead (your or your advisor's time spent on VAT, invoicing, OSS reporting).

Checkout & Payment Gap: based on Baymard Institute, Klarna, and PayPal benchmark data for cart abandonment by payment-method coverage. High-ticket orders (>€500 AOV) are 2–3× more sensitive to BNPL availability than low-ticket. We multiply your annual revenue by a payment-gap percentage, weighted by AOV.

Compliance Drag: based on average German Steuerberater rates (€80–€200/h) plus founder-opportunity-cost on time spent reconciling OSS, invoices, cross-border VAT. A Vendor-of-Record platform absorbs this entirely; DIY setups absorb the most.

What this isn't: a precise audit. It's a directional estimate from public benchmarks and 500k+ vendor data. Real numbers depend on category, region, and margin — but the gaps are real, and most operators are off by an order of magnitude when guessing.
