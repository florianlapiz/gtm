01 / 05 ICP Concentration

Look at your last 10
serious conversations — how similar are they?

ICP-drift is the silent killer at Pre-Seed and Seed. If every conversation looks unique, you don't have a repeatable motion — you have ten custom projects pretending to be a company.

02 / 05 Signal vs Volume

Are you reaching out
on timing signals — or volume?

At Pre-Seed, outbound isn't a sales motion — it's a research motion. The founders who win it are the ones who only contact accounts with an active trigger. The ones who lose it run volume.

03 / 05 Stack-Phase Fit

Is your GTM stack
right-sized for your phase?

A €1k/month HubSpot Enterprise setup at Pre-Seed is a slow death. So is a Clay subscription before 200 accounts/month. The right stack at the right phase is leverage. The wrong one is friction with a price tag.

04 / 05 Founder Mode

Could the next deal close
without you in the room?

At Pre-Seed, you should be in every conversation — that's where learning lives. At early Seed, you should be carving out a path so the first hire can carry standard motion. Most founders confuse the two.

05 / 05 PMF Reality

If you raised price 30%,
who would aggressively churn?

The real PMF test isn't NPS or retention curves. It's pricing pain. Customers who don't push back on price either love the product or aren't paying enough attention. You need to know which.

What this measures — and what it doesn't

Five levers, two points each, ten total. The score isn't a grade — it's a phase diagnostic. Founders at the same ARR can sit in different tiers depending on how systematized their motion is.

0–3 · Foundation Phase — pre-PMF or unclear ICP. The unlock isn't scaling outbound, it's customer learning. Founder should be in every conversation, treating pipeline as research.

4–7 · Finding Repeatability — system is emerging but doesn't yet carry the founder. Two or three levers usually decide whether the next 6 months land you at Series A or stall before it.

8–10 · Ready-to-Scale — repeatable motion, signal-driven outbound, lean stack, first hire delivering. Now the move is vertical expansion and ROI per hour, not adding inputs.

What we don't claim: this is a substitute for a real GTM audit. It's a calibration — same five levers from the Inovexus workshop, now self-serve. The strategic plays are operator moves from real founder engagements, not templates.
