How is this calculated?

Short version: we estimate the gap between the reach and revenue your current setup produces, and what a fully amplified, always-on creator program would produce at the same spend.

Why this gap exists: creator posts decay within 24–48 hours organically. Whitelisting (Meta) and Spark / Partnership Ads (TikTok / Instagram) let you put paid media behind that content as the creator. Industry data shows whitelisted creator ads outperform standard brand ads by 2–3x ROAS and reach 5–15x more audience at a fraction of repeat-creator-fee cost.

Inputs we use: your monthly spend, whether you currently amplify (full / partial / none), and whether your cadence compounds. Always-on programs build creative libraries and audience signals that one-off bursts don't.

What we don't claim: precision to the dollar. This is a directional estimate based on Meta Business case studies, TikTok Marketing Science, Tubular Labs, and Insider Intelligence benchmarks. Real numbers depend on category, creative, and offer.
